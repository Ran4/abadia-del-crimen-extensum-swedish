# Layout of "Tryck för att starta" (GLOSSARY §8) in pulsa.png. Read by tools/images/build_pulsa.py.
#
#   <name> en|pl <x0> <x1> gap <n> [shade <x>,<y>=<k> ...]
#                                    reuse the shipped ink in columns x0..x1 of
#                                    work/jar/res/<lang>/intro/pulsa.png (rows 158..192);
#                                    x0..x1 are the Phase A ink boxes (images/glyphs/index.json),
#                                    widened where noted to take in the glyph's own faint
#                                    anti-alias pixels (k < 8) next to the box. 'shade' re-shades
#                                    some of the glyph's own ink pixels (source x,y; hex k 1..F),
#                                    or drops one from the glyph with k 0 (a neighbour's pixel at
#                                    the edge of a widened box; it becomes opaque outline)
#   <name> grid <file> gap <n>       a hand-drawn glyph from this directory
#   <name> grid <file> on <dx>       a mark: its ink-left goes dx columns right of the previous
#                                    glyph's ink-left; it does not advance the line
#   gap = empty ink columns (ink = shade k >= 8, like the Phase A boxes) between the previous
#   glyph and this one: 0..2 inside a word, 6..7 between words (en/es/pl measure 0..2 and 6..7).
#   The glyph's ink-left goes to the previous glyph's ink-right + 1 + gap, so a negative gap
#   overlaps the two ink boxes: that tucks a glyph under an overhang (the r under the T's arm).
# The builder centres the whole line on x = 160.
#
# Joins inside words. In the shipped lines most letters of a word are linked by faint hairlines
# (k 1..5) at the feet or tops: en "start" s-t ("E26F"), a-r ("F52C") and r-t ("E19") at the
# top and t-a ("557") at the feet, en e-s ("B31E") and pl a-c ("C277") at the feet; some pairs
# are not (en r-e, es P-u, r-a; the r's foot never joins). Here, all from the font's own pixels: r-y (the y's head), c-k (the c's own foot tail),
# o-r and a-t (the r's head-serif hairline of en "start", as a-r there), t-t and the whole of
# "starta". Not joined: T-r (by design, see the r), y-c (the y's right stem is a descender), c-k
# at the top, f-o (the f's foot is the r's, which never joins).
#
# Word 1: "Tryck"

                                  # "Tr" is: its ink-left (the head serif, en 119) 3 columns left
                                  # of the arm's end, so only (near-)opaque outline separates the
                                  # T's hairline from the r: 2 columns beside the serif, 3 beside
                                  # the stem (P and r even touch in en "Pr"). At gap 1 the
                                  # space under the arm was a transparent pocket that showed the
                                  # chair rope of monje.png as a grey dash inside the T.
                                  # Left out on purpose: the r's own faint head-serif pixels at
                                  # en 118 (k 2 at row 172, k 6 at row 173); taken in, they would
                                  # join the r to the T. The outline takes their place.
                                  # NOT widened to 126 (the flag's faint tip, k 1/7): with the y
                                  # at gap 1 it would land on the y's head and join them with no
                                  # outline between ("DF75FF5"). The tip becomes outline, so this
                                  # r's flag is 1 px blunter.
y      grid y.txt       gap 1     # hand-drawn from es u
c      pl 127 132       gap 1     # pl "Naciśnij" c (the only c of the font), with its own column
                                  # 132: the foot's upturned tail ("4" over "2", rows 180..181,
                                  # after the foot's "C") and the top's flick ("2", row 172, which
                                  # in pl touches the ś). Here the tail's "2" meets the k's stem
                                  # foot ("6"), so c and k are joined at the foot ("FFC26FFFC"),
                                  # like pl a-c ("FFC277"); the flick stays 1 px clear of the k.
k      grid k.txt       gap 1     # hand-drawn from es l + en r flag

                                  # at the f. The o's ink-left is then its left stroke (en 167),
                                  # and gap 2 sets the stroke 2 columns after the f's crossbar
                                  # (en "to": the o's stroke 1 column after the t's crossbar).
dots   grid odots.txt   on 0      # ö = o + two upright 2x2 dots over the o's two strokes
                                  # (on 0: the first dot over the left stroke; see odots.txt)

                                  # head-serif hairline (k 2, row 172), the a-r join of en "start"
                                  # ("F52C"); here it joins o-r at the top ("822C"). The serif's
                                  # other pixel, en (205,173) k 6, is dropped (shade 0): next to
